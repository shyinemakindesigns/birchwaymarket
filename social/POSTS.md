```
Nobody hires a production designer for the master.
They hire for the eight sizes that come after it.

That work is invisible when it's done right, so this case study makes the production the deliverable: one holiday key visual for a fictional grocer, Birchway Market, adapted to 8 IAB, Meta and DOOH specs, plus an animated HTML5 banner.

The art is built in four layers (background, pie, produce, garnish). Every size re-crops the same vectors instead of redrawing them. That's the clip.

The part worth arguing about is QA. The checklist reads the rendered file instead of trusting a tick box, and the first draft failed on its own evidence: logo 4 px from the edge, CTA contrast at 1.78:1 against a 4.5:1 minimum. Cream on gold looked warm and readable. The math disagreed.

Also inside: file naming and versioning, a workload board, platform copy checked against character limits, and an SEO, AEO and GEO plan.

Where does your production process still run on eyeballing?

#CreativeProduction #DesignOps #Accessibility #HTML5Ads #PortfolioProject
```
